# presentation of a group -- wikipedia

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presentation_of_a_group

- In mathematics, a presentation is one method of specifying a group.
  A presentation of a group G comprises a set S of generators --
  so that every element of the group can be written as a product of powers of some of these generators --
  and a set R of relations among those generators. We then say G has presentation < S | R >

- Formally, the group G is said to have the above presentation
  if it is isomorphic to the quotient of a free group on S
  by the normal subgroup generated by the relations R.
